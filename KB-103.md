# KB-103: Payment Verification

**Category**: Payments

## 1. Missing Payment Verification
For pending bank transfers, customer must supply exact Payment Transaction Reference ID (UTR).

# KB-104: Mobile Plans & Roaming

**Category**: Mobile

## 1. 5G Data & Roaming
Enable international roaming via mobile app settings. Default APN is `internet.nexmobile.com`.

# KB-105: Escalation Procedures

**Category**: Support Operations

## 1. Physical Cable Damage
When outdoor construction or physical cable severance occurs, escalate directly to Level-2 Field Splicing Technicians with Handover Summary.
