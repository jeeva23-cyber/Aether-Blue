# KB-102: Broadband Connection Troubleshooting

**Category**: Technical Diagnostics

## 1. WAN Light Indicators
- **Solid Green**: Physical line active and connected to exchange.
- **Blinking Red**: Optical signal disruption or authentication in progress.
- **Off / Red**: Cable disconnected, physical fiber damage, or line fault.

## 2. Power Cycle Sequence
1. Turn off router power for 30 seconds to discharge power.
2. Verify optical fiber cable is securely clicked into yellow/blue WAN port.
3. Power back on and wait 3 full minutes for WAN light to turn solid Green.
